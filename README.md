# Market Sim

A small market simulation in the browser, set up to behave like XAU/USD (spot
gold against the US dollar). Prices are not generated by a formula: they come
from orders meeting in an order book, the way they do on a real exchange.

Plain HTML and JavaScript, a canvas candle chart, no backend and no dependencies.

## Run

Open `index.html` in a browser.

## How it works

- `js/engine.js` is the market: a limit order book with price-time priority.
  Makers rest limit orders, takers hit them, and the price is simply the last
  traded price. Your Buy eats the cheapest sell orders and raises the price;
  your Sell does the opposite.
- One tick is one second of market time. Trades are grouped into 1-minute
  candles, and the bigger timeframes merge those same candles.
- The price is in USD per troy ounce and moves in steps of $0.01.
- Order size is typed in lots. One lot is 100 oz, so a $1 move is $100 per
  lot. The smallest order is 0.01 lot (1 oz). Profit and loss are in USD.
- You start with a fake balance of 10,000. No fees.
- Two markets, picked in the left panel. **Forex** (the default): 1:1000
  leverage, and you can sell first and buy back later. A margin call closes
  the position when equity falls below half its required margin.
  **Stock**: cash only, and you can only sell what you own.
- **Stop loss / take profit**: two prices on your position. When the market
  trades there, the position is closed with a market order.
- The market is open from Sunday 23:00 to Friday 22:00 GMT. The clock jumps
  over the weekend and the chart shows no candles for it.
- The simulation starts on a Monday at 00:00 GMT and the bots trade among
  themselves until Wednesday 13:00, so there is history when you arrive.

## Settings

`js/config.js` holds the values that define the market.

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `startPrice` | `'live'` fetches the current XAU/USD price when the simulation starts. Put a number there to start from that price instead. |
| `offlinePrice` | Used only when the live price cannot be fetched. |
| `tickSize` | 0.01 |
| `lotSize`, `minLot` | 100 oz per lot, smallest order 0.01 lot |
| `startBalance` | 10,000 |
| `leverage`, `stopOut` | 1:1000, margin call at 50% |

Everything that was tuned to make the bots produce a gold-like market
(session activity, spreads, sizes, how often each bot acts) is in the `TUNING`
block at the top of `js/bots.js`.

## The bots

Every bot has its own account and places real orders through the same order
book as you (`js/bots.js`). None of them can set the price. How far the price
moves in a day comes out of how often they trade, how big their orders are and
how much is waiting in the book.

| Bot | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Random (300) | Buys or sells at random, 1 to 2 percent of its balance each time. Half hit the market, half wait as limit orders around the price. Each one acts rarely. |
| Trend follower (4) | Fast SMA above slow SMA means buy, below means sell. |
| High frequency trader (2) | Keeps a buy just below the price and a sell just above, and moves them as the price moves. The gap between them is the spread. |
| Whale (1) | Almost always idle, then one huge market order, 10 to 50 times the normal size (a normal order is 10 oz). |

**Sessions (GMT)** change how often the bots act and how big their orders are:
Asia 22:00 to 07:00 is thin and quiet, London 07:00 to 13:00 is busier, 13:00
to 17:00 (London and New York together) is the busiest, New York 17:00 to 22:00
slows down again. The high frequency traders quote wider in the quiet session
and right after a sudden move.

**News** comes 2 to 4 times a week, at 13:30 GMT on a weekday. For two and a
half hours the whale trades one way, hardest in the first ten minutes, and the
trend followers trade five times as often. The chart shows a News flag where
each event began.

## Headless run

```
npm run simulate
```

Runs the same market with no screen for 30 simulated days and prints how
gold-like it came out: the average daily range, the spread and volume in each
session, every news move, and whether the weekends stayed closed. Each line
says `ok` or `MISS` against its target.

```
node tools/simulate.js --days 10 --seed 7 --price 3900
```

`--days` sets the length, `--seed` picks another random run, and `--price`
starts from a fixed price instead of the live one.

## Tests

```
npm test
```

Runs the engine, position, calendar, news, indicator and bot tests with Node's
built-in test runner. Nothing to install.

## Controls

- **Market**: Forex or Stock.
- **Time speed**: how many ticks run per real second (1× to 600×), plus Pause.
- **Timeframe**: 1m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h, 4h.
- **Chart**: drag to pan, scroll to zoom, double-click to jump back to the latest candle.
- **Drawing tools**: pick a tool, then drag on the chart (or click once for each end).
  Click a drawing and press Delete to remove it. Escape cancels.
- **Indicators**: SMA and EMA with your own period.
- **Stop loss / Take profit** (right panel): fill them in before you place an
  order, or edit them while a position is open. Empty means none.
